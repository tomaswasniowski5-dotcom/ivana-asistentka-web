Why not fortune-telling

Responsibility for your life stays with you

Tarot can be used for fortune-telling. But the human psyche doesn’t handle that approach well – waiting for a predicted event shifts responsibility for your own life somewhere else. That’s why, at Pure Well, tarot serves only as a tool that reveals where your power lies: in how you perceive a situation, in the decisions you make, and in what you create and influence.

Tarot brings clarity to context and direction – not fate.

The cards as a mirror

Not an authority that decides for you

Within this framework, the cards act as a projective mirror. Much like projective methods in psychology, they bring up associations, images and stories that reflect your current state of mind, your unconscious patterns and your inner conflicts.

The approach draws on Jungian psychology and the concept of projection, grounded in more than 600 years of symbolic tradition. I bring in coaching and mentoring techniques so that the conversation turns into concrete steps.

How a session works

From question to action

Mapping your themes

You don’t need perfectly worded questions. Just come with an open mind and a sense of what’s weighing on you right now – much like in therapy. Together, we’ll turn it into concrete questions for the reading.

The reading

What’s happening around you and why, what’s happening within you, the environment you’re moving in, the intentions and dynamics at play in the relationships and situations around you – and what consequences your decisions may have.

Recap and next steps

Philosophising can be great as a brainstorm, but real situations call for real steps. Every session ends with a clear overview of what you’re taking away and what you’ll do with it.

PDF summary

After the session, you’ll receive a summary of the main themes, insights and agreed steps that you can come back to anytime.

Sessions take place live online, from the comfort of your home. The only requirement is a quiet space for the whole reading.

Who it’s for

For women and men at a crossroads

For anyone seeking clarity, a name for their patterns, or concrete direction in relationships, work or the course of their life.

This isn’t a session for those who want to know what will happen. It’s for those who want to understand what’s happening – and what to do about it.
